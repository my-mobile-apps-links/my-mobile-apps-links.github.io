Privacy Policy

PDF Studio (iOS & Android, com.pdf.editor.pdfeditor)
Effective date: October 29, 2026

PDF Studio is a PDF toolkit: view, merge, split, compress, convert PDFs to and from images, extract text, organize pages, add watermarks, password-protect/unlock, and edit document metadata. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. In short: the app has no account and never asks for your name, email, phone number or contacts. Your documents are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase hides them.

1. Your documents

  • All PDF work — viewing, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, text extraction, page organization, watermarking, and password protection — happens locally on your device.
  • Your files are never uploaded, transmitted or shared with us or any third party. The app has no server.
  • Files you create are saved to your device (your Downloads / Documents folder, or your photo library for exported images) only when you choose to save or share them.

2. Advertising and tracking

  • The free app displays ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Privacy Policy).
  • AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and other data to serve ads and to measure their performance. On iOS, the app asks for your permission first through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and honors your choice. You can also opt out of personalized ads in your device's privacy settings.
  • In some regions (for example the European Economic Area and the UK) you may be shown a Google consent form to choose how your data is used for ads.
  • You can remove ads at any time with the one-time Remove Ads purchase; ad-free users do not receive ad-related data collection.

3. In-app purchases

  • The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). The app never sees or stores your payment details.
  • Your purchase status is stored locally on your device so the app remembers it, and it can be restored from the store after reinstalling.

4. Permissions

  • Files: the app accesses only the files you explicitly select, to process them.
  • Photos: used only to save the images you export with the PDF-to-Images tool.
  • Tracking (iOS): used only to ask whether you allow ad personalization, as described above.

5. Data we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK of its own. It does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, or the content of your documents.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
